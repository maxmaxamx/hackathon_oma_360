## Brand & Style

This design system defines an uncluttered, high-utility digital ecosystem. It delivers architectural clarity, spatial calm, and purposeful efficiency by stripping out visual noise, superfluous ornamentation, and decorative clutter.

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Modern homeowners, architects, construction managers, interior designers, and DIY builders seeking an effortless, transparent procurement and project ecosystem.
- **Tone & Mood:** Authoritative, clean, contemporary, and reliable. The experience evokes the precision of an architectural drafting sheet merged with seamless modern commerce.
- **Design Movement:** Clean Modernist Minimalism. The interface relies on generous white space, strong typographic hierarchy, subtle hairline borders, and confident color discipline. The signature geometric OMA mark—paired with large, bold '360' typography—anchors the brand across clean horizontal planes.

## Colors

The palette centers on the exact royal blue of the graphic identity, punctuated by a high-energy orange secondary accent for conversions, alerts, and vital focal points. Surfaces remain crisp, airy, and predominantly white or near-white to maintain maximum contrast and breathing room.

### Palette Strategy
- **Primary Royal Blue (`#2B59C3`):** The foundational brand color. Represents technical authority, precision, and structural stability. Used for active navigation states, primary buttons, identity marks, and dominant interactive anchors.
- **Secondary Orange (`#FF5A00`):** An energetic contrast tone. Deployed deliberately for immediate conversion triggers (e.g., primary checkout actions, urgent alerts, key metrics) without overwhelming the layout.
- **Tertiary Green (`#10B981`):** Functional indicator reserved strictly for verification badges, completed milestone stages, and in-stock confirmations.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `canvas-bg` (`#FFFFFF`): Main backdrop establishing maximum purity and brightness.
  - `surface-subtle` (`#F8FAFC`): Soft alternating background for grouped sections and card backgrounds.
  - `surface-border` (`#E2E8F0`): Hairline boundary for cards, input dividers, and structural table splits.
  - `neutral` (`#1A1D20`): High-contrast, near-black ink for readable, accessible body and title typography.

## Typography

The typography uses **Inter**, creating a neutral, modern, and highly legible text canvas that puts data, product specs, and imagery at the forefront.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Logo Pairing
- **The Brand Lockup:** The OMA geometric mark is paired with bold, heavy tabular numerals (`360`) set at matching height, conveying technical stability and completeness.
- **Numeric Clarity:** Numbers across pricing, measurements, and SKUs must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned columns.
- **Display Weights:** Headlines utilize heavier weights (`700` and `800`) with subtle negative letter-spacing to reinforce architectural precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a calm, spacious fluid grid system with controlled maximum page widths (1440px desktop max-width). White space is deliberate, uncluttered, and generous, ensuring complex operations feel lightweight.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Margins
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid, `gutter-desktop` (32px), `margin-desktop` (48px). Sections breathe with `space-2xl` padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` (16px), `margin-tablet` (32px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter` (16px), `margin` (16px). Grids collapse into vertical single columns or 2-column minimalist product cards.

### Structural Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm relies on ample distances (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between logical modules to avoid cramped UI density.
- Components use `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) inner paddings, keeping cards open and legible.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a minimalist aesthetic with zero decorative clutter, visual depth is achieved primarily through **low-contrast outlines** and **flat surface contrasts** rather than layered blurry dropshadows.

### Hierarchy & Depth Levels
- **Canvas Base:** Pure flat `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Surfaces & Panels):** Subtle light grey `#F8FAFC` fill or pure white card with a razor-sharp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadows are omitted entirely in static states.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Selectable Items):** Subtle lift using a diffuse ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 89, 195, 0.06)`, keeping the elevation clean and tinted with brand royal blue.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Modals & Floating Triggers):** Clean white surface with `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` and bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Geometry is clean and architectural, avoiding aggressive bubble curves or blunt razor edges.

### Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Elements & Standard Controls (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Buttons, inputs, search fields, chips, and table cells.
- **Cards & Content Panels (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Product cards, calculation containers, and structural sections.
- **Avatars & Count Pills (`full` / 9999px):** Account header icon, status dots, and numerical pill badges.

## Components

### Header (Ultra-Minimalist)
- **Structure:** Clean fixed or sticky container, 64px height on desktop (56px on mobile), background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Left Content:** OMA graphic mark paired with bold '360' typography in matching royal blue `#2B59C3`.
- **Center Area:** Completely open and clear. No cluttering links, promotional banners, or complex search bars.
- **Right Content:** Single minimal account icon (circular button 40x40px, subtle border or soft icon graphic in `#1A1D20` with royal blue hover state).

### Footer (Minimal)
- **Structure:** Single horizontal boundary separated by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border, background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`, generous top/bottom padding (`space-xl`).
- **Content Flow:** Horizontal line-up (or stacked vertically on mobile):
  1. Small OMA 360 logo identity on the left.
  2. Copyright notice in muted `#64748B` (`body-sm`).
  3. Essential legal hyperlinks: "Privacy Policy" and "Public Offer" in `#64748B` with hover transition to `#2B59C3`.

### Buttons
- **Primary (Royal Blue):** Solid `#2B59C3`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 44px height, font `label-lg`. Hover: `#2046A3`.
- **Accent (Orange Action):** Solid `#FF5A00`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius. Used for key conversion moments (e.g., "Submit Order", "Instant Pay"). Hover: `#E04F00`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1A1D20`. Hover background `#F8FAFC` and border `#2B59C3`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#2B59C3`, zero border, subtle hover highlight.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields:** Height 44px, white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, 4px corner radius. Focused state shifts border to royal blue `#2B59C3` with zero thick glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18x18px, subtle border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#2B59C3` (or `#FF5A00` for transactional agreement terms) and crisp white check icon.

### Cards
- **Product & Content Containers:** Clean white background `#FFFFFF`, boundary `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 8px radius. Generous internal spacing (`space-md`), high contrast typographic layout, and no unnecessary decorative shadows.
